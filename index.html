Mission allocation

50% of voluntary mission contributions and 50% of net profits from DailyBread+ subscriptions are committed to helping people experiencing homelessness and visiting hospice patients who have no family or loved ones available to visit them.

Operations and owner income

The remaining 50% supports costs related to operating DailyBread+, including storage, hosting, AI, narration, payment processing, maintenance, security, and future development. Any funds remaining from that operating portion after expenses may be retained by the owner as income.

Important: DailyBread+ is currently operated as a for-profit project and is not a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Voluntary contributions are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations. Supporting the mission does not unlock app features; app subscriptions and mission support are separate.

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel monthly support?

Yes. Recurring payments can be canceled through Stripe. Contact us if you need help locating your subscription details.

Is this tax-deductible?

No tax-deductible status is claimed. DailyBread+ is currently a for-profit project, not a registered nonprofit.

Who can I contact?

Email dailybreadplus.support@gmail.com for payment questions, cancellations, or support.
