Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

One-time contributions

One-time voluntary contributions are generally final. If a payment was made by mistake, was duplicated, or was unauthorized, contact us promptly and we will review the request.

Monthly support

You may cancel monthly support at any time. Cancellation stops future charges but does not automatically refund a charge already processed. Contact us if you need help canceling or believe a charge was made in error.

How to request help

Email dailybreadplus.support@gmail.com with the email used at checkout, the payment date, the amount, and a brief explanation. Do not email full card numbers or card security codes.

Processing time

Approved refunds are returned through Stripe to the original payment method. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment provider and financial institution.
